Calculate Max Bid

Bid formula: Maximum purchase price = the lowest of (1% rent-rule max all-in, 70% of ARV, or $65,000 all-in) minus rehab and other costs. ARV below $80,000 or vacant land = do not bid. A $40,000 all-in number is also shown as your preferred zone.
If your pasted auction text says “opening bid,” “starting bid,” or “minimum bid,” the app compares it automatically with your calculated maximum.
